Separate the receipts

Each document starts out as one receipt. If it holds several, turn on Multiple receipts and point at the pages: click one, or press and drag along the strip to take a run of them, then Done. Add the next receipt and carry on. Every page wears a bubble for the receipt it belongs to, and a page can wear two — that is two till slips scanned onto one sheet. Rotate a page with ↻, open it full-size with ⛶, and use ✕ to leave a blank or duplicate page out of the claim altogether. Rotating and ignoring take effect at once, no save needed.
